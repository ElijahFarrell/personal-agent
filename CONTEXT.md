# Shiloh AI — Domain glossary

Terms used on the marketing site and in client conversations. Implementation lives in code; this file is language only.

## Conversions

**Primary conversion** — The single action the homepage is designed to drive. For the marketing homepage, this is completing the **Free AI Audit** (guided questionnaire → personalized report).

**Secondary conversion** — The next-best action when someone is ready for human conversation without finishing the audit. **Book a strategy call** (calendar booking).

**Supporting touchpoints** — On-site AI chat and other paths that warm visitors toward primary or secondary conversion; not counted as the homepage north star.

## Offers

**Free AI Audit** — Lead magnet: visitor answers intake questions and receives a personalized report (automation opportunities, time savings, action plan). Entry at `/audit`.

**Strategy call** — Live discovery or scoping conversation with the founder/team; booked via external calendar link.

## Audience

**Homepage visitor** — Founder or owner-operator at an SMB (or small team). Primary mindset: skeptical of AI hype, time-poor, wants proof that AI saves time or money before committing to a build or a call. Ops and “AI-curious” profiles may appear in supporting sections but are not the hero voice.

## Positioning

**Brand promise (homepage)** — Consulting-first: Shiloh ships production AI systems, not slide decks or hype. Education shows up as supporting proof (audit report, chat clarity) that scoping and delivery are grounded in the visitor’s business — not as a separate “course” or content-first identity.

## Marketing presentation

**Visual direction (marketing site)** — Light, warm editorial: off-white / cream surfaces, charcoal typography, **terracotta** as the primary accent (CTAs, key links, badges — not purple). Approachable and advisor-like for founder visitors; platform detail sits below the fold, not in the hero aesthetic.

**Homepage structure** — Medium-length conversion page: hero (audit-led) → outcomes proof → how engagements run (short) → testimonials → compact founder credibility → final CTA. Platform catalog, agent showcase, and deep industry proof live primarily on secondary routes (e.g. Services), not as a long scroll on home.

**Hero message** — Outcome-led: where AI saves real time and money for the visitor’s business, then Shiloh helps ship it. Primary button invites the Free AI Audit; subhead bridges audit (education/scoping) to consulting delivery (platform or existing stack).

**Founder credibility (homepage)** — Ex-Microsoft / Fortune 10 background appears in the compact founder section, not in the hero or the outcomes metrics strip. Home proof emphasizes client results (hours, revenue, projects); personal résumé supports trust after interest, without enterprise-only signaling above the fold.

**Chat widget & audit (marketing)** — Same behavior as today; visual reskin only for the light editorial theme (terracotta accents, light surfaces). No new audit-promotion prompts or funnel changes in chat.
