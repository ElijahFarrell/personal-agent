# Hook Formulas & Openers
## The Golden Rule of Hooks
The hook must immediately tell the reader what they will receive. Never start with context, backstory, or setup. Start with the conclusion, the bold claim, or the pattern interrupt.
A bad hook makes the reader ask "so what?" A good hook makes them ask "how?"
---
## Hook Formula Categories
### 1. The Bold Claim Hook
State a counterintuitive or surprising truth as the opener.
**Formula:** `[Counterintuitive claim] — here's why.`
**Examples:**
- "The AI market isn't saturated. The commodity layer is. There's a difference."
- "Cold outreach is dead. Here's what replaced it."
- "Most AI agents fail in production. Not because of the model — because of the context."
### 2. The Number Hook
Lead with a specific, surprising number.
**Formula:** `[Specific number] [surprising result/fact].`
**Examples:**
- "73% of six-figure AI entrepreneurs started with a free project."
- "Average AI deal size grew 340% in one year — from $4,120 to $13,870."
- "The SaaS sector lost $2 trillion in market cap in 60 days."
### 3. The Story Hook
Open with a moment, not a summary.
**Formula:** `[Specific scene or moment that drops the reader into the story]`
**Examples:**
- "I sat down to work on my side project at 9pm. By 9:15 I had convinced myself the market was too saturated. Again."
- "A 22-year-old with no coding experience made $65,000 building voice AI. Here's exactly how."
### 4. The Question Hook
Ask a question the reader is already asking themselves.
**Formula:** `[Question that names their exact frustration or desire]`
**Examples:**
- "What's the highest-leverage thing you can work on right now?"
- "Why does every AI agency look exactly the same?"
- "What would you build if you knew it would work?"
### 5. The Contrast Hook
Show the gap between two realities.
**Formula:** `[Old reality] vs [New reality]. Most people are still living in the first one.`
**Examples:**
- "Old way: you use software to do work. New way: software does the work for you. Most businesses are still in 2022."
- "No-code tools: anyone can copy your workflow in an afternoon. Custom code: your system is your moat."
### 6. The Credibility Hook
Lead with a result or credential that earns the right to be heard.
**Formula:** `[Specific result or position] — here's what I learned.`
**Examples:**
- "After 3 years building AI systems at Amazon, here's what most AI tutorials get wrong."
- "I built an agent that writes my LinkedIn posts for me. Here's the architecture."
### 7. The List Hook
Promise a specific, numbered list of value.
**Formula:** `[Number] [specific things] that [specific outcome].`
**Examples:**
- "5 AI agent patterns that actually work in production."
- "3 reasons your AI agent fails when it leaves your laptop."
---
## Hook Length Rules
- LinkedIn post hook: 1-2 lines max. Must fit before the "see more" cutoff.
- Newsletter subject line: Under 50 characters. Curiosity or specificity — pick one.
- Outreach DM hook: Under 38 characters for the preview line (Tripwire approach).
- Long-form opener: 1 paragraph max. Get to the point by sentence 3.
---
## Hook Testing Checklist
- [ ] Does it make the reader ask "how?" or "tell me more?"
- [ ] Does it avoid starting with "I" or "We"?
- [ ] Is it specific — not vague or generic?
- [ ] Would you stop scrolling for this?
- [ ] Does it promise something the rest of the content delivers?
