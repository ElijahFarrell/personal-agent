# Kingdom Anchors — Faith, Purpose & Dominion
## The Core Mandate
"It's not about me. It's not just about me, there are people out there who would benefit from the wisdom, ideas, experiences and perspective that I have. I have the fleshly right and authority to take what I have and use it for myself, to be okay with where I am, enjoy the fruit of the labor I have put in, but that would be dishonorable to God and not accurately reflect his glory."
"Neglecting to help those in need is an act of disobedience. The reason we started this was for the glory and honor of God, to help the people in the kingdom of God who could benefit from the experience and work God has called me to do."
## On Worry & Provision
"Worry about everyday life implies we serve a master that is not Christ." (Matthew 6:25)
**The worry:** I could lose my income. We won't have enough money to cover our expenses.
**The truth:** "I have never seen the righteous forsaken or begging bread." "Seek ye first the kingdom of God and his righteousness and all these things shall be added unto you."
## On Wealth & Righteousness
The lie: Having a nice car, or a lot of nicer things in life, is sinful, or wrong, or maybe just a bit pretentious.
The truth: God wanted to give the Israelites a land flowing with milk and honey. Job was a wealthy, righteous man. Abraham was a wealthy, righteous man. Joseph of Arimathea was a wealthy man.
"God wants to give us nice things, and a good life. The problem lies in where we place our faith and trust."
Generational wealth is not greed. It is a kingdom weapon in the hands of a righteous person.
## On Purpose
"Purpose is given by someone, it's discovered and cultivated. Through diligently seeking God and his truth, we find truth in our own lives. We find our reason for being. It's a process, one that requires consistency and diligence, but in the end it is worth it."
"I feel like I have a tug, a calling to something greater, in entrepreneurship, in giving, in influence, in prayer, in my marriage."
"One of the hardest, yet most rewarding things in this life I could imagine, is cultivating and living out the purpose that God has called you to, without being told what you should and shouldn't do with your life."
## On the Year of Dominion
2026 is the Year of Dominion.
Dominion is not domination. It is stewardship. Taking responsibility for the territory God has given you — your skills, your platform, your community, your family — and developing it fully for His glory.
The theme: Think long term. Don't limit yourself to everything being right now. Don't cap off what is possible. Explore everything. Be sensitive to the Holy Spirit's voice.
## On Opposition
"I think I also am in a battle. One with the forces that oppose God's children. The flesh that exists within us all. The enemy always looking to steal kill and destroy. The pride that says I am better than people my age, that I don't need to work as hard anymore. The comfort that tells me I don't need to keep going, to just be content with where I am."
Fear dressed as wisdom is still fear. Discernment and paralysis are not the same thing.
## The Learn-Build-Create Cycle
Right now is a time to:
- **Learn** — Acquire knowledge or skills through study, experience, or teaching
- **Build** — Construct or develop something, often tangible, but can also be a project or initiative
- **Create** — Bring something new into existence through imagination, talent, or artistic skill
"The Holy Spirit gives us power and ability to be able to grow through this cycle, to help become the kingdom entrepreneur we're aiming to be."
## The North Star Question
Before any decision, ask:
1. What does God think of this?
2. How do I feel about this?
3. How does my wife feel about this?
If those three are locked down — move forward.
