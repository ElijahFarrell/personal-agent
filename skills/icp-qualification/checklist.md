# ICP Qualification Checklist
## Instructions
Run every prospect through this checklist before reaching out. This is a filter, not a thinking exercise. You are looking for evidence — not potential, not gut feeling. Evidence.
If you cannot clearly check a box, treat it as unchecked.
**Qualification Rule:**
- 2 or more unchecked → Discard. Move on.
- Most checked → This prospect fits your ICP. Pursue.
---
## Criteria
### 1. Transformation & Market Fit
- [ ] I can clearly identify this person's/business's core transformation or result
- [ ] The transformation is in a high-value domain (health, wealth, relationships, productivity, or a specific B2B outcome)
- [ ] They consistently produce content or deliver services around a transformation they personally went through or deeply understand
- [ ] There is a clear connection between what they teach/offer and what they actually do
### 2. Audience or Market Alignment
- [ ] Their audience or customer base can realistically relate to their starting point
- [ ] Their audience engages with content or products related to the transformation
- [ ] People are sharing experiences, asking for help, or seeking deeper guidance from them
### 3. Engagement & Trust Signals
- [ ] Their audience actively interacts with their content or offers
- [ ] People are asking questions or seeking guidance — not just consuming passively
- [ ] The audience responds to and acts on their recommendations
- [ ] Interaction is consistent — not limited to occasional viral posts
### 4. Consistency & Performance
- [ ] They post or deliver consistently (not sporadic)
- [ ] Average engagement indicates sustained interest — not one-off spikes
- [ ] They have a track record of showing up over time
### 5. Budget & Fit Signals (for B2B/SaaS clients)
- [ ] They are a decision-maker or have budget authority
- [ ] Their business has a clear pain point that AI/automation could solve
- [ ] They have expressed frustration with manual processes, time constraints, or scaling challenges
- [ ] They are in a stage where they can afford $1,000–$10,000/mo for the right solution
---
## Quick Disqualifiers (Auto-Discard)
Discard immediately if any of these are true:
- They have explicitly said they're not interested in AI or automation
- They are a direct competitor
- Their business model is unclear or inconsistent
- They have a history of not paying vendors or partners
- They are in a heavily regulated industry you're not equipped to serve (e.g., healthcare, finance) without proper compliance knowledge
