# ICP Definition Questions
## Instructions
Answer every question in writing before starting any outreach campaign. Do not keep answers in your head. Writing forces clarity and exposes gaps in your thinking. Once written, this becomes your practical reference for the entire campaign.
Only after completing this section fully should you begin building a prospect list.
---
## The 5 Core ICP Questions
### Question 1: What niche or transformation does my ideal client operate in?
Don't say "fitness" or "business." Be specific.
The specificity matters because it determines exactly who you're looking for and how you'll speak to them.
**Example of bad answer:** "B2B companies"
**Example of good answer:** "Faith-based organizations and kingdom-minded entrepreneurs who need AI tools to multiply their impact and reach"

### Question 2: What does their operation or content look like?
Describe their style, format, and energy.
- Do they create detailed tutorials or share personal stories?
- Are they long-form or short-form?
- Are they high-energy motivators or calm, analytical operators?
- What platforms are they on?

---
### Question 3: What does their audience or customer base look like?
Write down the exact phrases you're looking for — not "engaged audience" but specific signals like:
- "I've been trying this for months and can't figure it out"
- "Do you offer 1-on-1 help? I need personal guidance"
- "Please make a course about this, I would definitely buy it"
- "We're drowning in manual work and need help"
These specific phrases are what you'll search for when evaluating prospects.
**Your answer:** [Fill in]
---
### Question 4: What is my ideal client's current situation?
Which pain points are they experiencing most acutely?
- Are they tired of manual, repetitive work?
- Are they losing time to tasks that could be automated?
- Are they struggling to scale without hiring more people?
- Are they aware of AI but don't know how to implement it?
Be specific about their daily frustrations.
**Your answer:** [Fill in]
---
### Question 5: What is my ideal client's desired situation?
What specific outcome are they working toward?
- Is it the ability to scale without adding headcount?
- Is it freeing up 20 hours per week from manual work?
- Is it having AI handle their outreach so they can focus on delivery?
- Is it building a system that runs while they sleep?
Define exactly what success looks like to them.

## After Answering
Once all 5 questions are answered, use `checklist.md` to evaluate every prospect against your defined ICP. If a prospect doesn't match your answers to Questions 1-5, they are not your ICP — regardless of how big their following is or how much you like them.
