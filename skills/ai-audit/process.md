# AI Audit Process & Methodology
## Week 1: Discovery
### Step 1: Stakeholder Interviews
Goal: Understand the business deeply before recommending anything.
**Key questions to ask:**
- What does a typical day look like for your team?
- Where do you spend the most time on repetitive tasks?
- What processes break down most often?
- What would you automate first if you could?
- What are your top 3 strategic goals for the next 12 months?
- What's the biggest bottleneck to growth right now?
- How many hours per week does your team spend on [specific process]?
- What tools are you currently using for [acquisition / delivery / support / admin]?
**Departments to cover:**
- Sales & Acquisition
- Customer Delivery & Fulfillment
- Customer Support
- Admin & Operations
- Marketing & Content
### Step 2: Ops Canvas Mapping
Map the full business operation across four zones:
**Zone 1 — Acquisition:** How do leads come in? How are they qualified? How are they converted?
**Zone 2 — Delivery:** How is the service or product delivered? What are the steps? Who does what?
**Zone 3 — Support:** How are customer questions and issues handled? What's the response time?
**Zone 4 — Admin:** What back-office work happens? Reporting, invoicing, scheduling, data entry?
For each zone, document:
- Current process (step by step)
- Tools used
- Time spent per week
- Pain points and failure modes
### Step 3: Identify Opportunity Areas
After mapping, flag every process that is:
- Repetitive and rule-based (high automation potential)
- Time-consuming relative to its value (high ROI potential)
- Error-prone when done manually (high quality improvement potential)
- A bottleneck to growth (high strategic value)

## Week 2: Solution Design
### Step 4: Build the Opportunity Matrix
Load `opportunity-matrix.md` and place every identified opportunity into the matrix.
Categorize by:
- **Impact:** Large or Small
- **Effort:** Quick Win or Big Swing
### Step 5: Prioritization Criteria
Score each opportunity against these six criteria:
1. **Opportunity Impact** — How much time/money does this save?
2. **Alignment with Strategic Goals** — Does this directly support their top 3 goals?
3. **Digital Transformation** — Does this move them toward a more modern operation?
4. **Technical Complexity** — How hard is this to build? (1-5 scale)
5. **Change Management** — How much will the team resist this change? (1-5 scale)
6. **Solution Cost** — What will it cost to build and maintain?
### Step 6: Build Opportunity Briefs
For each top-priority opportunity, write a one-pager covering:
- **Problem:** What is the current pain?
- **Solution:** What AI/automation solves it?
- **How it works:** Simple explanation of the system
- **ROI:** Time saved per week × hourly cost = annual savings
- **Implementation timeline:** How long to build and deploy?
### Step 7: Executive Presentation
Structure:
1. Executive Summary — 3 key findings
2. Current State — Ops Canvas overview
3. Opportunity Matrix — visual prioritization
4. Top 3 Recommendations — opportunity briefs
5. ROI Summary — total estimated savings
6. Proposed Next Steps — what engagement looks like

## Common AI Opportunities by Department
### Sales & Acquisition
- AI-powered lead qualification (score leads automatically)
- Automated CRM updates from call recordings
- AI follow-up sequences for lost leads
- Database reactivation campaigns
- AI sales call analysis for coaching
### Customer Delivery
- Automated client onboarding workflow
- AI progress tracking and check-in distribution
- Client success dashboard with engagement tracking
### Customer Support
- AI support triage chatbot (RAG over knowledge base)
- Automated FAQ responses
- Escalation routing based on sentiment analysis
### Admin & Operations
- Automated meeting transcription and action items
- AI-generated weekly reports
- Automated scheduling and calendar management
- Invoice and contract automation
