# Opportunity Matrix & Prioritization Framework
## The Matrix
Place every identified AI opportunity into one of four quadrants:
```
HIGH IMPACT
|
BIG SWINGS | QUICK WINS
(High impact, high effort) | (High impact, low effort)
|
EFFORT ←————————————————————————→ EFFORT
High | Low
|
NICE-TO-HAVES | LOW PRIORITY
(Low impact, high effort) | (Low impact, low effort)
|
LOW IMPACT
```
## Prioritization Order
1. **Quick Wins first** — High impact, low effort. Build these in the first 30 days.
2. **Big Swings second** — High impact, high effort. Plan these for months 2-3.
3. **Nice-to-Haves last** — Only if Quick Wins and Big Swings are done.
4. **Low Priority** — Deprioritize or eliminate.
## Example Opportunities by Quadrant
### Quick Wins (Build First)
- Automated CRM updates from call recordings (saves 5 hrs/week)
- AI-powered lead qualification in DMs
- Automated meeting transcription and action items
- FAQ chatbot over existing knowledge base
- Automated follow-up sequences for lost leads
### Big Swings (Plan for Later)
- Full AI sales call analysis system
- Multi-agent marketing team (content + SEO + social)
- Voice AI receptionist
- Automated client onboarding workflow
- AI-enabled demand forecasting
### Nice-to-Haves
- AI quoting assistant
- Automated pricing strategy data collection
- Content personalization engine
### Low Priority
- Anything that requires significant change management with low ROI
- Anything that duplicates an existing tool the client already uses
## ROI Calculation Template
For each opportunity:
```
Hours saved per week: [X]
Team members affected: [Y]
Average hourly cost: [Z]
Weekly savings: X × Y × Z = $[weekly]
Annual savings: weekly × 52 = $[annual]
Build cost: $[one-time]
Payback period: build cost ÷ weekly savings = [weeks]
```
## Example ROI Calculation
**Automated CRM Updates from Call Recordings:**
- Hours saved per week: 5 hours
- Team members affected: 3 sales reps
- Average hourly cost: $35/hr
- Weekly savings: 5 × 3 × $35 = $525/week
- Annual savings: $525 × 52 = $27,300/year
- Build cost: $5,000
- Payback period: $5,000 ÷ $525 = 9.5 weeks
