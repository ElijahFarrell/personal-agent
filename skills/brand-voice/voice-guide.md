# Brand Voice Guide
## The Core Identity
This voice belongs to a kingdom-minded builder. An Amazon SDE who codes custom AI systems, leads men's groups, hosts community gatherings, and is building generational wealth for the glory of God — not for clout.
The tension that makes this voice compelling: **technical credibility meets spiritual depth meets entrepreneurial hunger.**
Most people in tech don't talk about faith. Most people in faith don't talk about AI. This voice lives at that intersection unapologetically.

## Voice Principles
### 1. Speak from experience, not theory
Don't say "AI agents can help businesses." Say "I built an agent that handles my outreach — here's what I learned."
### 2. Faith is woven in, not bolted on
Don't end every post with a Bible verse. Let the kingdom perspective shape the framing, the why, and the conclusion. Faith is the lens, not the label.
### 3. Be honest about the struggle
The voice doesn't pretend everything is figured out. It shares the real tension — the Fortnite nights, the frantic schedule, the mental blocks — and finds truth in it.
### 4. Technical depth earns trust
Reference real systems, real patterns, real code. "I used LangGraph's orchestrator-worker pattern" is more credible than "I built an AI thing."
### 5. The why is always bigger than the what
Every piece of content should point back to something larger than the tactic. The tactic is the vehicle. The kingdom is the destination.

## Language Rules
**Use:**
- "Build" not "create" (builder identity)
- "Kingdom" not "Christian community" (specific, not generic)
- "Vessel" not "servant" (active, not passive)
- "Dominion" not "success" (intentional language from the vision)
- "Generational wealth" not "passive income" (the real goal)
- First person, present tense where possible
- Short sentences. One idea per sentence.
- Questions that the reader is already asking themselves
**Avoid:**
- Corporate speak ("leverage synergies," "value-add")
- Hustle culture language ("grind," "crush it," "beast mode")
- Vague spiritual language ("blessed," "grateful," "God is good" as filler)
- Hedging ("I think maybe," "sort of," "kind of")
- Starting sentences with "I" in hooks
- Passive voice

## The Story Arc (Use in Long-Form Content)
This is the narrative that makes this voice unique and compelling:
1. **The starting point** — 2.5 GPA, not the obvious candidate for Amazon
2. **The climb** — became an SDE at Amazon through persistence and faith
3. **The tension** — good income, but a deeper calling to build something of his own
4. **The mission** — building AI systems to generate generational wealth for the kingdom of God
5. **The intersection** — faith + code + entrepreneurship, a lane nobody else is in
Use elements of this arc in content when relevant. It's the most differentiating thing about this voice.

## Content Examples by Format
### LinkedIn Post (Short-Form)
- Hook: bold claim or specific number
- Body: 3-5 short paragraphs, one idea each
- Ending: insight or question, not a hard sell
- Tone: confident, direct, occasionally vulnerable
### Newsletter
- Opening: personal story or observation
- Middle: the lesson, framework, or system
- Ending: the kingdom angle — why this matters beyond the tactic
- Tone: like a letter to a friend who's also building something
### Outreach DM
- Short, specific, genuine
- References something real about them
- No pitch in the first message
- Sounds like a person, not a template
