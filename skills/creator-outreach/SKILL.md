# Creator Outreach Skill
## When to Use This Skill
- Reaching out to Instagram creators or LinkedIn prospects
- Writing initial DMs, follow-ups, or audit offers
- Handling responses (enthusiastic, cautious, or skeptical)
- Moving a prospect from cold contact to booked call
- Personalizing outreach at scale
## Core Outreach Philosophy
Templates are frameworks, not scripts. Personalize every message based on the specific person. The moment outreach feels generic is the moment it stops working.
Three principles:
1. **Stay genuine** — don't put on a persona. People can tell.
2. **Position with authority** — speak confidently about what you deliver, not arrogantly.
3. **Be persistent, not pushy** — follow up consistently. Ignore their responses or boundaries and you've lost them.
## Profile Requirements (Before Any Outreach)
Before sending a single DM, the profile must be ready:
- Professional profile picture (face, not logo)
- Clear bio stating what you do: "I help [X] achieve [Y]"
- Social proof in highlights or pinned posts
- Real posts showing you do this work
- No age in bio
## Instagram Rate Limits (Critical)
- Maximum 30-40 DMs per day
- Spread messages throughout the day — never batch send
- Take one day off per week to prevent spam flagging
## The 5-Step Outreach Sequence
Load `dm-templates.md` for all message templates.
Load `follow-up.md` for follow-up timing and sequences.
**Step 1:** Initial message (Story Reply or Tripwire Question)
**Step 2:** Follow-up if no response (3-4 days later)
**Step 3:** Audit offer (after they reply)
**Step 4:** Send the Loom audit
**Step 5:** Follow up on Loom + move to call
## Response Handling
Three response types — each requires a different approach:
- **Type 1 (Enthusiastic):** Respond fast, keep commitment low (15 min call), make it easy to say yes
- **Type 2 (Curious but cautious):** Give brief concept, explain why call > DMs, no pressure framing
- **Type 3 (Objection/Skeptical):** Never argue in DMs. Acknowledge, validate, redirect to call
