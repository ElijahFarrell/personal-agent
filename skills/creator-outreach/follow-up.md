# Follow-Up Sequences & Timing
## The Follow-Up Philosophy
Following up is not being annoying. It is being serious. The difference:
- **Following up** = consistent, respectful, adds value each time
- **Pushy** = ignoring their responses or boundaries, repeating the same message
Most deals are closed on the 3rd-5th touchpoint. Most people stop at 1.
## Timing Guide
| Step | Action | Wait Time |
|---|---|---|
| Step 1 | Initial DM | Day 0 |
| Step 2 | Follow-up if no reply | Day 3-4 |
| Step 3 | Audit offer (after reply) | Same day they reply |
| Step 4 | Send Loom | Same day they agree |
| Step 5 | Follow-up on Loom | Day 2-3 after sending |
| Step 6 | Call booking follow-up | Day 1-2 after calendar sent |
## Key Principles
- **Reply quickly** when they respond — while they're still engaged
- **Add new value** in each follow-up — new observation, new angle, not just "bumping this"
- **Keep commitment low** — always suggest 15 minutes, not an hour
- **Make it easy to say yes** — use a calendar link, not "what time works for you?"
- **One follow-up per step** — don't double-message within the same step
## When to Stop
Stop following up after:
- They explicitly say no or not interested
- 2 follow-ups with zero response after the Loom
- They've gone cold for 2+ weeks after showing interest
Move them to a "re-engage in 30 days" list, not the trash.
