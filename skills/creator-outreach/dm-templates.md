# DM Templates — Full Outreach Sequence
## STEP 1: Initial Message
### Approach A: Story Reply
Reply to their Instagram story with a genuine observation and a relevant question. Contextual, not cold.
**Template 1 — Content Observation:**
"Hey [Name], saw your story about [specific thing they posted]. Seems like your audience keeps asking about [pattern you noticed]. How do you handle [relevant question about their process]?"
**Template 2 — Milestone Acknowledgment:**
"Hey [Name], congrats on [specific milestone/achievement]. I noticed you're [observation about what they do]. Are you doing anything with [related opportunity area]?"
### Approach B: Tripwire Question
Under 38 characters including spaces. Makes them think you're a potential customer — gets you into their primary inbox.
**Template 3:** "Do you offer 1-on-1 coaching?"
**Template 4:** "Are you coaching clients?"
**Template 5:** "Do you have a coaching offer?"
---
## STEP 2: Follow-Up (No Response After 3-4 Days)
**Template 1 — Simple Bump:**
"Hey [Name], just bumping this - curious if you saw my message?"
**Template 2 — Add Context:**
"Hey [Name], not sure if you saw my last message. I noticed [specific new observation about their content]. Still curious about [original question]?"
**Template 3 — Different Angle:**
"Hey [Name], saw you just posted about [recent content]. That actually relates to what I was asking about earlier - [reframe your original question]?"
---
## STEP 3: Audit Offer (After They Reply)
**IMPORTANT:** Only create the audit AFTER they agree to see it. Use Ghostwriter + Loom to record it.
**Template 1 — Standard:**
"That sounds great. I actually went ahead and created an audit for your page. Basically, I analyzed your content, your audience engagement, and where I see opportunities for you to monetize without disrupting what you're already doing. I recorded a Loom walking through everything - would it be cool if I sent that over to you?"
**Template 2 — Problem-Focused:**
"Got it. I actually put together a quick breakdown for you. Went through your content and spotted a few gaps between what you're posting and what you could be monetizing. Made a Loom walking through what I'm seeing - want me to send it over?"
**Template 3 — Opportunity-Focused:**
"Nice. I actually made a video breakdown for you showing some opportunities I spotted in your content. Looked at your engagement patterns and where your audience is already asking for help. It's about [X] minutes - cool if I send it?"
---
## STEP 4: Sending the Loom
**Pro tip:** Upload Loom to YouTube first — boosts credibility.
**Template 1 — Simple:**
"Here's the audit I mentioned - it's about [X] minutes. Let me know what you think."
**Template 2 — With Context:**
"Here you go - this is the breakdown I put together for your page. About [X] minutes. Let me know your thoughts."
**Template 3 — With Specific Call-Out:**
"Here's the video. I focused specifically on [one key thing you covered]. It's about [X] minutes. Curious what you think."
---
## STEP 5: Follow-Up on Loom (No Response After 2-3 Days)
**Template 1 — Simple Check-In:**
"Hey [Name], just bumping this - curious what you thought of the breakdown I sent over?"
**Template 2 — Acknowledge Busy:**
"Hey [Name], I know you're probably swamped. Did you get a chance to check out that video? Would love to hear your thoughts."
**Template 3 — Add New Value:**
"Hey [Name], following up on that audit. Also noticed [new observation about their recent content] - ties into what I showed in the video. Lmk if you watched it."
---
## STEP 6: Moving to a Call
### Type 1 Response — Enthusiastic
**Template 1 — Direct:**
"Glad it was helpful. I'd love to share more about how I can help you implement all of this. Would you be against hopping on a quick 15-minute call? Let me know what time works & I'll pencil something in."
**Template 2 — Calendar Link:**
"Awesome. Let's hop on a quick call to discuss next steps. Here's my calendar - grab a time that works for you: [calendar link]"
**Template 3 — Acknowledge Interest:**
"Really glad this resonated with you. Happy to walk through exactly how we'd implement this together. Got 15 minutes this week? Here's my availability: [calendar link]"
### Type 2 Response — Curious but Cautious
**Template 1 — Brief + Redirect:**
"Happy to explain more. Basically, I'd help you [core value prop]. But rather than explain everything through DMs, it would make more sense to jump on a quick call so I can understand your specific situation and walk you through how it would work for you. Would you be open to that? 15 minutes, and if it doesn't seem like a fit, no worries at all."
**Template 2 — Answer + Redirect:**
"Great question. [Brief 1-2 sentence answer]. That said, there's a lot more to it depending on your specific setup. Would you be down for a quick 15-minute call where I can understand your situation better and walk through exactly how this would work for you? No pressure - if it's not a fit, totally fine."
**Template 3 — Customization Angle:**
"I totally get wanting more details. The thing is, how this works really depends on your audience, your content, and what you're already doing. Rather than give you a generic explanation through DMs, let's jump on a quick call where I can understand your situation and show you exactly what this would look like for you specifically. Sound good?"
### Type 3 Response — Objection or Skeptical
**Template 1 — Time Objection:**
"Got it. That's actually one of the reasons this works well - you'd stay focused on creating content while I handle the [service] side. But rather than explain everything through DMs, would you be open to a quick 15-minute call where I can show you what the process actually looks like? Then you can decide if it's something that fits into your schedule."
**Template 2 — Past Bad Experience:**
"I totally get that - when partnerships don't work out, it makes you more cautious. On a call, I can walk you through how I approach things and what makes it different, and you can decide if it feels like a better fit than what you experienced before. No pressure, just a conversation."
**Template 3 — Audience Doubt:**
"That's actually something we'd figure out together on a call. I can show you some patterns I've already noticed in your audience from the audit, and we can talk through whether there's a real opportunity there. 15 minutes, and if it doesn't seem like it would work, no worries at all."
**Template 4 — Feels Too Small:**
"That's actually not uncommon - a lot of [creators/businesses] think they need to be bigger before they can [benefit]. But based on what I saw in your content, your audience is already asking the right questions. Would you be open to a quick call where I can show you specifically what I'm seeing? Then you can decide if the timing makes sense."
### No-Show on Booking
"Hey [Name], just wanted to make sure you got the calendar link. Let me know if any of those times work for you."
